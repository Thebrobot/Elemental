“Body contouring” means different things to different people: **fat reduction**, **muscle stimulation**, **skin tightening**, or **pelvic floor support**. At Elemental Skin & Body in **Elkhorn, WI**, we offer several device categories; the right choice depends on your anatomy, medical history, and what you want to feel in the mirror or in daily life.

## Muscle and definition: Emsculpt Neo®

[Emsculpt Neo](/services/body/emsculpt-neo) combines electromagnetic stimulation with radiofrequency heating to target muscle contraction and adjacent fat in selected areas. It appeals to patients who already train or want adjunct support for the abdomen, buttocks, arms, or thighs, as always, after candidacy review.

## Pelvic floor and confidence: Emsella®

[Emsella](/services/body/emsella) uses high-intensity focused electromagnetic energy to stimulate pelvic floor muscles, often discussed in the context of stress incontinence or postpartum recovery. It is a **non-invasive, fully clothed** option for appropriate candidates.

## Minimally invasive tightening: FaceTite & BodyTite

For patients considering **radiofrequency-assisted lipolysis** with more dramatic skin contraction than non-invasive devices alone, [FaceTite](/services/body/facetite) and [BodyTite](/services/body/bodytite) may be part of the discussion. These are different from external applicators; downtime and expectations are reviewed in detail at consultation.
