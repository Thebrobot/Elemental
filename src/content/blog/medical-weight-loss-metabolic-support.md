Interest in **medical weight loss**, including GLP-1–based therapies, has grown dramatically. A responsible program is more than a prescription; it includes **screening, monitoring, nutrition and lifestyle guidance**, and clear expectations about side effects and plateaus.

## Why medical oversight matters

Our [medical weight loss](/services/wellness/medical-weight-loss) visits begin with history, medications, and often **labs** so the plan fits your metabolism and risk profile. Not every patient is a candidate for every medication; contraindications and drug interactions are taken seriously.

## Labs and hormones in context

Weight change rarely happens in isolation from sleep, stress, thyroid, and sex hormones. We may recommend [wellness labs](/services/wellness/wellness-labs) or discuss [BIO-ID hormone replacement](/services/wellness/bio-id-hormone-replacement) when symptoms and testing suggest an imbalance. These are separate decisions from weight medications but often part of a whole-person plan.

## Supporting therapies

Some patients add [IV therapies and peptides](/services/wellness/iv-therapies-peptides) or [B-12 / MIC injections](/services/wellness/b12-mic-injections) for energy and adherence, always after review of your goals and medical history.
