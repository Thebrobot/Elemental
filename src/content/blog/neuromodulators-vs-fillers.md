If you are new to injectables, the first decision is not *which brand* to choose; it is whether your concern is driven by **muscle movement** or **volume and structure**. Neuromodulators (such as Botox® and Jeuveau®) and [dermal fillers](/services/injectables/dermal-fillers) solve different problems. Many patients eventually use both, but they are not interchangeable.

## When a neuromodulator is the right tool

Neuromodulators target **dynamic lines**, the ones that appear when you frown, squint, or raise your brows. Treatment relaxes specific muscles so the skin creases less deeply with expression. Common areas include the forehead, glabellar “11” lines, and crow’s feet.

At [Elemental Skin & Body](/), we map your muscles and discuss how much movement you want to keep. The goal is a refreshed version of you, not a frozen or unfamiliar look. Learn more about our approach on the [Botox & Jeuveau](/services/injectables/botox-jeuveau) page.

## When fillers (or biostimulators) make sense

Fillers address **volume loss**, contour, and deeper folds that are visible **even at rest**. Cheeks, lips, jawline, and nasolabial areas are typical discussion points. Some products add immediate volume; biostimulators encourage your tissue to build collagen over time.

If your lines are mostly from movement, filler alone will not solve the underlying cause, and vice versa. That is why a face-to-face assessment matters.

## Combining treatments safely

Neuromodulators and fillers are often combined in a thoughtful plan. **Sequencing and timing** depend on the areas treated; we coordinate same-day or staged visits when appropriate. If you are also interested in skin texture or tightening, options such as [microneedling](/services/skin/microneedling) or [Morpheus8](/services/skin/morpheus8) may be part of the conversation.
