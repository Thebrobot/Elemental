# Image assets - alt text conventions

All **content** images (hero, team, treatment photography) must:

- Describe **what is in the frame** and **why it matters** to the visitor (context), not generic strings like “image1”.
- Avoid implying **medical outcomes** unless copy is legally approved (no “after results” in alt text unless paired with compliant labeling).
- Stay roughly **under 125 characters** for screen-reader comfort.
- Use **empty alt** (`alt=""`) only for **decorative** graphics (dividers, purely ornamental shapes) that duplicate adjacent text.

Examples:

- Hero: `Bright treatment suite at Elemental Skin & Body in Elkhorn, Wisconsin, with soft daylight and neutral decor.`
- Treatment card: `Consultation discussion at a med spa desk; papers and skincare samples are visible but not legible.`
- Decorative texture: `alt=""` with `aria-hidden="true"` on the wrapper.

When replacing placeholders in this folder, prefer **original photography** or **licensed stock** with usage rights for commercial medical marketing.
